## From Founder to Healthcare Infrastructure

### TheraMatch

I helped turn clinical fit, provider data, and patient preferences into a working matching product, then carried its product and commercial story through acquisition and integration with TPN.health.

### TPN.health and TPN Match

At TPN.health, I led product and technical work across the systems that connect provider access to reimbursement. My scope included:

- provider-network data, participation, care navigation, matching, and payer rules;
- eligibility roster intake and real-time 270/271 coverage checks;
- provider-ledger invoices, fee schedules, pricing, repricing, claim creation, and corrected claims;
- 837 claim transactions, TA1/999/277CA acknowledgments, 276/277 status, 835 remittance, and denials;
- separate payer and provider ledgers and remittances, ACH/NACHA, treasury and settlement controls, posting, and reconciliation; and
- controls for retries, duplicate prevention, failure recovery, audit evidence, and human approval.

[TPN.health reports](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/30/3319865/0/en/tpn-health-introduces-proprietary-integrated-claims-and-credentialing-platform-resolves-patient-barriers-to-behavioral-health-access-and-ensures-clinician-payments.html) that its platform grew from about 25 claims per day to capacity above 5,000. That is a company result; my work covered product and technical systems behind it.

## How I Build

- Models draft or propose work. People or policy approve actions.
- Each action keeps a record of what was requested, what happened, and what remains unknown.
- Tests cover the handoffs where money, claims, or customer trust can fail.
- A result means an outcome we can prove, not a forecast.
